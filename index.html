About

Geography at the College of Staten Island

Cary Karacas teaches geography at the College of Staten Island, City University of New York. He holds a PhD in geography from the University of California, Berkeley, and is co-editor of Cartographic Japan: A History in Maps (University of Chicago Press, 2016).

This site hosts map quizzes, videos, and other materials for his courses. Official grades and course records are kept in Brightspace.
